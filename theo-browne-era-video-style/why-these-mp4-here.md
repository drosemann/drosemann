# why-these-mp4-here.md

### Why are 15.mp4 files in my GitHub profile repo?

This folder `theo-browne-era-video-style` is my video archive.

After 2000+ gaming videos, I tried 15 videos in Theo Browne style – fast-paced tech takes, rants, and explainers. I took them down from YouTube and archived them here on purpose.

Not because I'm quitting or going back to one specific format. I archived them here to figure out what kind of videos I actually want to make in the future – Theo Browne style again, pure tech rants, or gaming.

This repo is my personal lab to review, compare and decide.

**Why are 7 videos compressed? Because Microsoft is stingy.**

GitHub = Microsoft. Trillion-dollar company, but still a 25 MB limit for browser uploads and 100 MB hard limit via Git push.
