### Why are 15 .mp4 files in my GitHub profile repo?

Short answer: This is my time capsule.

This folder `theo-browne-era-video-style` contains 15 videos from my Tech-Content Phase inspired by Theo Browne. Quick cuts, hot takes, dev rants – my attempt at doing tech content differently after 2000+ gaming videos.

**Why are they HERE and not just on YouTube?**

1.  **Archive, not content graveyard.** I deleted my old gaming videos once. Won't make the same mistake again. YouTube compresses, deletes, demonetizes. Git keeps history.
2.  **Proof of work.** These 15 videos are learning-by-doing. Editing, storytelling, audio, pacing. Not perfect, but proof that I tried a completely different style.
3.  **Yes, MP4s in Git is bad practice.** I know. Looking at you, Git LFS. This is my profile repo, not a production codebase. It's supposed to be personal.

**Why I'm closing this chapter:**

I'm going back to my **Gaming Video Phase**.

The Theo Browne era taught me a lot about how to explain tech, but Gaming is where I started in 2018 with ShadowUnity and where I have the most fun. I want to build where my community actually is.

These videos stay here as a marker. From `Aternos -> KVM` and from `Gaming -> Tech -> Gaming again`.

If you found this folder via my profile: grab some popcorn, these are unlisted and raw.

— Daniel
